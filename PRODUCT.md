# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Primary audience is B2C home decor customers looking for curated products and inspiration.  
Secondary audiences are B2B professionals (decorators, partners), suppliers/artisans, and the internal admin team managing catalog, orders, and content.

## Product Purpose
Art Home Deco is a premium e-commerce and inspiration platform for interior decor.  
Its purpose is to balance fast purchase for straightforward products with qualified contact capture for personalized decor projects.  
Success means users can quickly understand the offer, buy confidently, or start a guided conversation when their need is custom.

## Brand Personality
Calm, premium, editorial.  
The tone should feel curated, confident, and warm without being loud.  
The interface should communicate trust, product quality, and design sensitivity while staying practical.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic marketplace aesthetics with noisy discount-driven UI, excessive badges, and visual clutter.  
Avoid over-animated, flashy, tech-style interfaces that reduce readability and product focus.

## Design Principles
1. Clarity first: every screen prioritizes legibility, orientation, and obvious next actions.
2. Editorial rhythm: use balanced spacing, strong hierarchy, and calm composition to guide reading naturally.
3. Product truthfulness: present products and information with precision, avoiding manipulative urgency patterns.
4. Dual-intent journeys: support both quick checkout and higher-touch contact/project flows without friction.
5. Operational continuity: visual improvements must preserve all business logic and admin efficiency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA across public and admin surfaces.  
Ensure keyboard navigability, visible focus states, and sufficient contrast for text and interactive elements.  
Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by minimizing non-essential animations and preserving functional transitions.
